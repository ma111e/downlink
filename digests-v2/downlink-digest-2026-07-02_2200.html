Claude Fable 5 isn’t permanently leaving subscriptions, Anthropic says

Anthropic says Claude Fable 5 will leave standard subscription access after July 7 and move to usage-credit billing, but the company says this is temporary. It plans to restore Fable 5 to subscriptions once capacity can handle demand.
KEY POINTS
  • 01Anthropic says Claude Fable 5 will not be accessible through Claude subscriptions after July 7, but the company expects the model to return to subscriptions once capacity allows.
  • 02Fable 5 was recently restored after the U.S. government lifted export controls on Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models.
  • 03Anthropic said Fable 5 will be included for up to 50% of weekly usage limits on Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise plans through July 7, after which it will be available through usage credits.
  • 04A Claude Code lead engineer clarified on X that Anthropic aims to restore Fable 5 as a standard part of subscription plans as soon as capacity allows.
  • 05Anthropic said demand for Fable 5 is expected to be very high and difficult to predict, so subscription-plan access is being rolled out conservatively in stages.

Anthropic says Claude Fable 5 will not be accessible through Claude subscriptions after July 7, but the company says this is not a permanent change. The model is expected to return to subscription plans when Anthropic has enough capacity. Fable 5 was recently restored after the U.S. government lifted export controls on Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models.

Anthropic had said Fable 5 would be available globally on Claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and the Claude Platform, but the company has restricted usage because of high demand. For Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise plans, Fable 5 is included for up to 50% of weekly usage limits through July 7. After that date, it will be available through usage credits.

A Claude Code lead engineer clarified on X that Anthropic aims to restore Fable 5 as a standard part of subscriptions as soon as capacity allows. Anthropic said demand for Fable 5 is expected to be very high and difficult to predict, so subscription access is being rolled out conservatively in stages. The company said Fable 5 is fully available today on the Claude API and consumption-based Enterprise plans, while users who rely on it through subscriptions should expect usage-credit billing after the deadline.

  • Users on Claude subscriptions who depend on Fable 5 should plan for usage-credit billing after July 7 even though Anthropic says the change is temporary.
  • Anthropic is treating subscription access more conservatively than API and consumption-based Enterprise access, where Fable 5 is described as fully available today.
  • The clarification from a Claude Code lead engineer was needed because Anthropic’s original wording led users to think Fable 5 might become a permanent paid upgrade outside regular subscriptions.
  • Anthropic is prioritizing earlier limited access over waiting for enough capacity to provide broader standard subscription access.

Anthropic says its Claude Fable 5 model will stop being included in regular Claude subscriptions after July 7 and will instead require usage credits, but the company says this is not permanent. People and organizations on Pro, Max, Team, and some Enterprise plans who rely on Fable 5 are affected because they may have to pay based on use after that date. The broader issue is that demand for Anthropic’s most powerful model is high and hard to predict, so the company is limiting access until it has enough capacity to make it part of subscriptions again.

Claude Fable relaunch disappoints users with nerfed performance

Anthropic’s restored Claude Fable 5 is available to more users but is heavily capped and often falls back to Opus 4.8 due to strict safety guardrails. Users say the result feels significantly weaker than the original release, especially for coding and security-adjacent tasks.
KEY POINTS
  • 01Claude Fable 5 is now available to all users, including Max, Pro, and Team subscribers, but Anthropic says it is heavily capped.
  • 02Anthropic says users can use Fable for up to 50% of their weekly usage limits, and after July 7 the model will transition entirely to a usage-credits system.
  • 03Users on Reddit report that the restored Fable 5 feels weaker or is routed through stricter safety systems more often than before.
  • 04Claude Code users report that tasks involving C, C++, Rust, Win32 API references, memory-related work, or words like “security,” “vulnerable,” “unsafe,” and “hook” can trigger a fallback or block.
  • 05BleepingComputer says the model itself has likely not been nerfed, but Anthropic’s extra safety guardrails and large safety margin are negatively affecting daily use cases.

Claude Fable, described as Anthropic’s most powerful model, is now available to all users after the Department of Commerce lifted its ban. It is included in Max, Pro, and Team plans, but Anthropic says it is heavily capped. Users can use Fable for up to 50% of their weekly usage limits, and after July 7 it will move entirely to a usage-credits system.

Early user reactions have been disappointing because the restored Fable 5 appears to behave differently from the original release. Reddit users report that it feels weaker or is routed through stricter safety systems more often, with some tasks falling back to Opus 4.8. BleepingComputer says it has also observed Fable sometimes being routed to Opus 4.8 even when the task did not appear to be a safety risk.

The impact is especially visible in Claude Code, where users report problems with systems-level coding work. One developer said references to C, C++, Rust, Win32 API, memory-related work, or words such as “security,” “vulnerable,” “unsafe,” and “hook” appeared to trigger a fallback or block. BleepingComputer understands that the model itself has not been nerfed, but that Anthropic’s extra safety guardrails and large “safety margin” may be negatively affecting daily use cases.

  • The main degradation appears to come from routing and safety controls rather than a weaker underlying Fable 5 model, according to BleepingComputer’s understanding.
  • Security-adjacent terms in code or project files, such as “security,” “vulnerable,” “unsafe,” or “hook,” can be enough to disrupt normal Claude Code workflows by triggering a fallback or block.
  • The fallback behavior is visible to users because Claude tells them when it switches to Opus 4.8, making safety-driven routing noticeable during work.
  • Fable 5’s availability is temporary in its current subscription-included form because Anthropic plans to move it entirely to usage credits after July 7.

Anthropic has made its Claude Fable 5 model available again, including to paid Claude users, but people are finding that it is tightly limited and often switches to a different model instead of completing their requests. This affects Claude users and developers using Claude Code, especially when working on programming tasks that mention security-related words or low-level code. The broader concern is that the extra safety limits may make a powerful tool much less useful for everyday work, even when the task does not appear risky.
