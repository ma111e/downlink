OpenAI is preparing “o,” an always-on ChatGPT assistant that could handle email

OpenAI is reportedly testing an unannounced always-on ChatGPT assistant called “o,” with leaked configuration references suggesting it may eventually have email capabilities. The company has not confirmed the feature, but more details could emerge at DevDay 2026.
KEY POINTS
  • 01OpenAI is reportedly testing an unannounced always-on ChatGPT assistant called “o.”
  • 02The phrase “o, your always-on assistant” briefly appeared among benefits for the $100 ChatGPT Pro plan.
  • 03A configuration reference lists the display name “o” and an email suffix of “-o,” suggesting the assistant may have some form of email capability.
  • 04OpenAI has not confirmed or denied that it is developing the always-on assistant.
  • 05OpenAI will host DevDay 2026 in San Francisco on September 29, where it is expected to announce new features.

OpenAI is reportedly testing an unannounced always-on ChatGPT assistant called “o.” The phrase “o, your always-on assistant” briefly appeared in benefits for the $100 ChatGPT Pro plan. OpenAI has not confirmed or denied that it is developing the feature.

The plan listing placed “o” alongside expanded Work and Codex usage, maximum memory, 100GB of file storage, and early access to new features. A dynamic configuration also referenced display_name: "o" and an email_suffix: "-o", which the article says could indicate an eventual email capability or identity. The article distinguishes “o” from the internally referenced Aeon system for custom ChatGPT workspace agents, and notes that OpenAI could reveal more at DevDay 2026 in San Francisco on September 29.

  • The “o” reference appeared as part of a higher-tier ChatGPT Pro package alongside expanded work and Codex usage, maximum memory, 100GB of file storage, and early-access benefits.
  • The configuration’s “-o” email suffix is a separate clue from the plan listing and points to a possible email-related identity or capability for the assistant.
  • The article distinguishes “o” from the internally referenced Aeon system, which is associated with custom agents for ChatGPT workspaces rather than a consumer-facing experience.

OpenAI appears to be testing an unannounced ChatGPT feature called “o” that would act as an always-available assistant, possibly including its own email capability. The company has not confirmed it, but references to it briefly appeared in the benefits listed for the $100 ChatGPT Pro plan. If released, it could help ChatGPT users continue tasks even when they are not actively using the service, while raising important questions about what access such an assistant would have to users’ work and communications.
