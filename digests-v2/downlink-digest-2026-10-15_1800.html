Administrators reported attacks beginning around September 10, before the formal advisory and CVE publication on October 14.

A Second Security study cited by the article found that the median time between public fixes and CVE publication was 39 days in August, up from 5.1 days in January. The researchers also found that public exploit code can emerge within hours of a security fix and before a corresponding CVE is published.
