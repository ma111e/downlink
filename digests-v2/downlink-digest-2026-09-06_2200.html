ChatGPT Astra is now rolling out to $20 Plus subscription

OpenAI is gradually rolling out its new flagship ChatGPT Astra model to $20 Plus subscribers, with some users seeing it first in ChatGPT Work rather than regular Chat. Astra is included within existing Plus limits, but OpenAI has not said when or whether free users will get access.
KEY POINTS
  • 01OpenAI is gradually rolling out ChatGPT Astra to users with a $20 ChatGPT Plus subscription.
  • 02Astra may appear in ChatGPT Work before it becomes available in the regular Chat model picker for some Plus users.
  • 03OpenAI said GPT-6 Astra is already available to all Pro, Enterprise, and Business Premium users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and is also live in the API.
  • 04OpenAI says Astra usage is included within existing subscription limits, with the option to buy credits for additional usage.
  • 05OpenAI has not said whether ChatGPT Astra will roll out to free users, who currently have access to GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.

OpenAI is gradually rolling out ChatGPT Astra, described as its most powerful model to date, to users with a $20 ChatGPT Plus subscription. The model may appear in ChatGPT Work before it becomes available in the regular Chat model picker. In the article’s example, Astra was available in the author’s Plus account under Work, but not yet in normal Chat.

OpenAI previously said GPT-6 Astra is available to all Pro, Enterprise, and Business Premium users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and that it is also live in the API. The company said it may take a few days to roll out to Plus and Business users. OpenAI says Astra usage is included within existing subscription limits, and users who need more usage will be able to buy credits.

Astra is OpenAI’s new flagship model and is designed for computer use, browsing, coding, science, cybersecurity, and longer professional tasks. OpenAI says it is significantly better than GPT-5.6 Sol at maintaining context during complex work. The company has not said whether Astra will roll out to free users, who currently have access to GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.

  • Plus users who do not see Astra in the regular Chat model picker may still be able to access it by checking the ChatGPT Work section.
  • The rollout is not uniform across ChatGPT surfaces, since one Plus account had Astra in Work while the normal Chat model picker still did not offer it.
  • Organizations or users needing Astra immediately may already have access through Pro, Enterprise, Business Premium, Codex, or the API rather than waiting for the Plus rollout to complete.
  • Plus subscribers do not need to change plans just to try Astra, because OpenAI says its usage is included within existing subscription limits.

OpenAI is starting to make ChatGPT Astra, its newest and most powerful model, available to people who pay for the $20 ChatGPT Plus plan, though it may appear first in the Work section before regular Chat. People with Pro, Enterprise, and Business Premium plans already have access, while free users do not yet know if or when they will get it. This matters because Astra is meant to be better at long and complex tasks such as browsing, coding, science, cybersecurity, and computer use, so access to it could affect what kinds of work people and businesses can do with ChatGPT.
