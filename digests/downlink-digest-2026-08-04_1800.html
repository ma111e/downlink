Netskope Threat Labs identified 28 malicious npm package versions published across the @servicetitan, @or-sdk, @onereach, and @umacloud namespaces on 2026-08-04.   • The same payload targets both developer workstation secrets and CI/CD environment secrets in one run, so the real-world impact depends heavily on where the npm install occurs.
  • The rapid publishing bursts across unrelated namespaces suggest the campaign was automated and involved more than a single isolated package compromise.
  • The article explicitly warns that the Ethereum RPC endpoints are legitimate public services abused by the payload, making them better suited for alerting than blind blocking.
