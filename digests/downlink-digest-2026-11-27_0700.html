Summary

The issue affects sites in unusual or incomplete installation states, including production sites, and may not be apparent through the Drupal user interface. The flaw addresses attack paths left after Drupal patched the earlier CVE-2026-132165 remote code execution issue, which attackers began exploiting one day after patches were released. Administrators should upgrade to Drupal 10.6.23, 11.3.23, or 11.4.14, or block access to /core/install.php if an immediate upgrade is not possible. Drupal recommends confirming control of the user 1 email address, reviewing logs for unexpected user 1 account activity, and following its compromise-response guidance where needed.

  • Drupal warns that a site already taken over may no longer appear vulnerable, so applying the fix does not by itself rule out an earlier compromise.
  • The earlier Drupal flaw began to be exploited one day after patches were released, illustrating the short time administrators may have to apply critical updates.
  • For organizations unable to update immediately, blocking access to /core/install.php is a specific temporary measure Drupal recommends for both new and existing sites.
