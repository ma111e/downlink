Kaspersky has attributed a new malware called Umbrij to the threat actor ToddyCat, describing it as a tool for covertly accessing victims’ Gmail-hosted corporate email through the Google API. The campaign targeted corporate email communications by abusing Google’s OAuth 2.0 authorization process to obtain tokens for email access.
