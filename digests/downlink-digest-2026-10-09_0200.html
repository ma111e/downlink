In plain words

At the Pwn2Own Ireland 2026 contest, researchers earned \(1.26 million for finding 98 previously unknown security weaknesses in products including Samsung and Google phones, OpenAI Codex, and Oracle’s AI database service. Ikotas Labs won the event, including a \)300,000 reward for taking control of a Google Pixel 10 through several linked flaws. Summary
Pwn2Own Ireland 2026 researchers earned $1.262 million for demonstrating 98 zero-day vulnerabilities across devices and AI products, with Ikotas Labs winning after exploits against the Samsung Galaxy S26, Google Pixel 10, OpenAI Codex, and Oracle Autonomous AI Database.

Pwn2Own Ireland 2026 ended with security researchers receiving \(1,262,000 for demonstrating 98 previously unknown security flaws. Ikotas Labs won the competition with 42.5 Master of Pwn points and \)361,000 in rewards after compromising the Samsung Galaxy S26, OpenAI Codex, and Oracle Autonomous AI Database. The team also received the event’s largest single reward, \(300,000, for using multiple linked flaws to compromise the Google Pixel 10. Xint placed second with \)240,000, while Team ZyGoat placed third with $125,000.

Twenty-nine research teams targeted products across seven categories, including mobile phones, AI infrastructure, AI coding applications, messaging apps, smart home devices, printers, and wellness healthcare devices. On the final day alone, researchers earned \(641,000 after demonstrating 21 zero-days, including further compromises of the Galaxy S26 and three compromises of the Pixel 10. Apple’s iPhone 17 was eligible for a maximum \)300,000 reward for a remote compromise, but no team registered an attempt. Under the contest rules, vendors have 90 days to fix disclosed flaws before Trend Micro’s Zero Day Initiative publicly releases technical details.

  • The final day accounted for $641,000 of the contest’s $1,262,000 total and included three compromises of the Google Pixel 10 plus another successful rooting of the Samsung Galaxy S26.
  • Although the Samsung Galaxy S26 was compromised on the first day, Samsung already knew some of the bugs used in those demonstrations.
  • Apple’s iPhone 17 offered a maximum $300,000 reward for a remote compromise, but no team registered an attempt.
  • The contest expanded beyond traditional devices to include AI infrastructure, AI coding applications, and wellness healthcare devices, while requiring targets to run current firmware versions.
