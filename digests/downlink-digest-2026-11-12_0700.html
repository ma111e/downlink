  • 03A confirmed trust-boundary violation requires more than seeing or authenticating to an internal service; the tester must obtain authorization and safely perform a controlled action outside the original boundary.
  • 04Service account credentials, network access, and runtime context must be evaluated together because an over-privileged credential can enable access to internal APIs even when network segmentation is strict.
  • 05Cloud pentests of provider infrastructure require separate authorization and should confirm cross-tenant effects only with controlled test tenants and synthetic data.
