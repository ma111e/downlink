Summary

The first escape attempt used recently disclosed bugs in the host kernel, including Januscape, CVE-2026-53359.   • The article shows that vulnerabilities not labeled as security bugs can still become part of a practical escape chain when an agent can connect them with other flaws.
  • Older stable distributions are presented as a concrete risk because their slower backport process left the author exposed to bugs already fixed or disclosed elsewhere.
