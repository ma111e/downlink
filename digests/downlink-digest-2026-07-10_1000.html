  • The impact extends beyond Alibaba because XQUIC is open source and may be embedded in any HTTP/3 server using the library with default QPACK settings.
  • The crash was caught by glibc’s _FORTIFY_SOURCE=2 in FoxIO’s release build, but the article says that without this check the same bug writes out of bounds past the end of the new buffer.
  • FoxIO says it attempted disclosure five times between April 7 and May 9 before publishing details, while the article states there was still no patch or CVE as of July 10.
